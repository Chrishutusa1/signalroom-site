# Season 2 Keyword Plan (approved by Chris, Nov 13, 2026)

Every Season 2 episode published by the `add-podcast-episode` routine (or by hand) must apply this
plan at publish time, on Buzzsprout, YouTube and signalroompodcast.com. Do not publish an episode
without checking its row below.

## Rules
1. Title leads with the primary keyword phrase, word for word, then the hook, then `| Guest Name`.
   Buzzsprout and YouTube use the same title. The site `<title>` must also lead with the phrase and stay <= 57 chars.
2. The primary keyword appears in the first sentence of the Buzzsprout description (first 100 characters).
3. Buzzsprout tags (max 255 characters, comma separated) start with: primary, secondary, then the
   Clinical AI / AI consulting tag when the row calls for it, then guest name and "The Signal Room".
   YouTube tags carry the same terms (max 500 characters).
4. "Clinical AI" in the description lede or "AI consulting" as a tag only when the guest actually
   discusses it in the recording (search the Buzzsprout transcript first). Never claim what the guest did not say.
5. Titles and descriptions go by what the guest says and does in the recording, not by the on-air intro.
6. If a target phrase is not tracked in Podseo (podcast 4650), add it to the keyword tracker.
7. Log every change in Neon public.change_log (project delicate-leaf-67982487).
