AI Leadership and the Future of Work

AI adoption changes how people communicate, learn, exercise judgment, and trust their leaders. The Signal Room's leadership interviews explore what happens inside organizations as those expectations shift, drawing on experience in coaching, organizational learning, healthcare, and enterprise work.

Dr. Angela C. Meyers discusses polymathy and AI leadership, examining the value of learning across disciplines. Katherine Tuominen explores workplace communication, and Diane Weaver considers trust between employees and leaders. Dr. Larry Kuhn examines the human side of leadership, while Arnaud Saint-Paul brings a perspective on self-governance and organizational responsibility.

The conversations offer ways to examine how an AI initiative affects the people expected to use it. Where does judgment sit? What becomes harder to discuss? How can leaders recognize changes in trust and learning? Use the source interviews to compare the guests' perspectives with your own setting. Leaders, researchers, and PR representatives can propose additional conversations grounded in specific organizational experience.
