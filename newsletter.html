Why The AI Health Pulse Exists

Healthcare leaders are drowning in AI content, and almost none of it is written for the person who actually has to govern, fund, or explain an AI decision. The AI Health Pulse was built for that reader. Each weekly edition distills signal from noise across clinical AI, data strategy, governance, reimbursement, and the operational reality of running AI inside hospitals, payers, and life sciences organizations. It is written by Chris Hutchins, founder of Hutchins Data Strategy Consultants and host of The Signal Room, and it is edited to the rule that if a sentence does not change how a leader thinks or decides, it does not belong.

What You Can Expect Each Week

Every edition follows a consistent rhythm so busy executives know exactly what they are getting. Expect a sharp opening read on a current healthcare AI development, a short analytical breakdown that separates hype from operational truth, a practitioner perspective drawn from a Signal Room guest or client engagement, and a forward-looking signal for the week ahead. Editions never exceed what a leader can absorb over coffee, and they are designed to be forwarded to a clinical team, an executive peer, or a board member without apology.

Who It Is Written For

The AI Health Pulse is written for CEOs, CMOs, CIOs, CDOs, COOs, CFOs, clinical and informatics leaders, and the vendors and consultants who serve them. When you subscribe you self-tag your role, and BeeHiiv delivers any role-specific segments to the right audience. Over time that means the newsletter gets sharper for the person actually reading it, not broader for the sake of mass distribution.

How It Connects to the Podcast

Many themes first appear in The AI Health Pulse and then deepen into full Signal Room episodes with the leaders shaping that conversation. If a newsletter edition triggers a question, the podcast is often where the extended answer lives. Subscribers frequently tell us they treat the newsletter as weekly signal and the podcast as monthly depth.
