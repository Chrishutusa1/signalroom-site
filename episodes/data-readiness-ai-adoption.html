Show Notes

AI readiness in healthcare is not a marketing slogan; it is a data readiness problem, and 80 to 85 percent of organizations are not prepared. Ratnadeep Bhattacharjee, co-founder and Managing Director of Tech Variable, has spent his career bridging non-technical leaders and deeply technical engineering teams so that data challenges become competitive advantages. His argument is direct: before an organization trains a model, it should audit whether its data can be trusted to train anything at all.

What We Cover

  • The 4-lens data audit framework every healthcare organization should run before deploying AI: completeness, consistency, connectivity, and compliance.
  • A real Medicaid health plan case study where fixing foundational data issues improved quality measures reporting without deploying a single AI model.
  • How Tech Variable's SyncMesh platform harmonizes fragmented data from EHRs, claims feeds, pharmacies, and devices into a unified FHIR-compliant patient 360 view.
  • Why data maturity must be treated as an ongoing service rather than a one-time project, and what that looks like operationally.
  • The 4 trends shaping healthcare AI through 2030: ongoing data maturity, context-aware AI, ambient AI designed to reduce burnout rather than add clicks, and ethics embedded by design.

Key Takeaways

Data readiness is the bottleneck, not model selection. Organizations that skip the audit and move straight to AI procurement end up with pilots that demonstrate nothing because the underlying data cannot support a defensible conclusion.

Interoperability is a governance outcome, not a technical feature. Healthcare data is fragmented across EHRs, claims, pharmacies, and devices. A unified patient 360 view requires data contracts and stewardship, not just a new integration platform.

Ethics belongs at every stage, not at the end. AI adoption that waits until deployment to ask ethics questions has already baked in the decisions that matter most. Ethics by design is cheaper and safer than ethics by retrofit.

Frameworks & Tools Mentioned

  • Four-lens data audit: completeness, consistency, connectivity, compliance
  • SyncMesh integration platform (Tech Variable)
  • FHIR-compliant patient 360 architecture
  • Context-aware and ambient AI patterns
  • Ethics-by-design adoption model

About Ratnadeep Bhattacharjee

Ratnadeep Bhattacharjee is co-founder and Managing Director of Tech Variable, a healthcare IT firm specializing in AI-driven solutions, data integration, and digital transformation. His perspective is distinctive: a non-technical founder in a deeply technical world, leading with curiosity and servant leadership, bridging engineering teams and healthcare stakeholders who care about outcomes, not algorithms.
