Why AI Governance Is a Human Problem

with Arnaud Saint-Paul · Founder & CEO, Tapuat

Everyone is busy governing the model; almost no one is governing themselves. Arnaud Saint-Paul argues that an organization has to be coherent before it scales AI—or it just automates its own dysfunction. Chris Hutchins presses that thesis into the place it matters most: a high-stakes, regulated environment like healthcare.

Show Notes

Everyone is busy governing the model; almost no one is governing themselves. Arnaud Saint-Paul argues that an organization has to be coherent before it scales AI—or it just automates its own dysfunction. Chris Hutchins presses that thesis into the place it matters most: a high-stakes, regulated environment like healthcare.

What We Cover

  • Arnaud's path—founding and exiting companies to building the Heart Leadership Institute
  • The thesis: self-governance and coherence as the prerequisite to scaling AI
  • The experiment—running a leadership assessment through several LLMs that disagreed
  • Human bias vs. machine bias, and the honest difference between them
  • The high-stakes test: a patient arrives with an AI-generated workup
  • Provenance, prompt visibility, accountability, and giving the clinician time back
  • The first move for a leader: establishing a coherence baseline

Key Takeaways

  • Govern yourself before you govern the model. Coherence—clarity of judgment, trust, and decision-making—has to come before AI is deployed at scale, or the tool just accelerates the dysfunction.
  • Model bias is real and varies by maker. Running the same human-judgment assessment through several leading models produced different answers, a caution against outsourcing judgment without provenance.
  • Healthcare is the stress test. When a patient arrives with an AI-generated workup, what matters is provenance, prompt visibility, accountability, and the rule that the tool has to give the clinician time back.

About Arnaud Saint-Paul

Arnaud Saint-Paul is Founder & CEO of Tapuat and its Heart Leadership Institute, and the author of Heart Leadership: Fostering Coherence to Accelerate Impact. A serial entrepreneur turned “conscious leadership architect,” he created the Heart Leadership Index to measure the human side of leadership—coherence, judgment, and trust. Learn more at tapuat.com or connect on LinkedIn.
