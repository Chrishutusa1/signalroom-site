Why AI Agents Are Healthcare's Next Biggest Security Risk

with Pranava Adduri · Co-Founder & CTO, Bedrock Security

AI agents don’t just answer questions — they take actions, call tools, and move data on their own. Bedrock Security’s Pranava Adduri makes the case that agentic AI is healthcare’s next biggest security risk, and that securing it starts with the data and identities underneath, not the model on top.

Show Notes

AI agents — systems that don’t just answer questions but take actions, call tools, and move data on their own — are arriving in healthcare faster than the controls meant to govern them. Pranava Adduri, Co-Founder and CTO of the data-security company Bedrock Security, joins Chris Hutchins to explain why agentic AI may be healthcare’s next biggest security risk: autonomous software that inherits broad access to PHI, operates under non-human identities no one is watching, and can move sensitive data at machine speed. The conversation is about what changes when the thing using your data isn’t a person — and what a data-centric approach to securing it actually looks like.

What We Cover

  • Agent vs. chatbot — what makes an “AI agent” different: autonomy, tool use, and the ability to act on data rather than just describe it
  • Agents inherit access — an agent can reach everything the identity behind it can reach, and most organizations have no map of what that is
  • Non-human identities — the machine accounts, API keys, and service tokens agents run on, and why they’re the least-governed part of the stack
  • PHI at machine speed — how autonomous systems can expose or move sensitive data far faster than a human could, and why detection has to keep pace
  • Secure the data, not just the model — a data-centric view of governance: knowing where sensitive data lives, who (and what) can touch it, and least-privilege for agents
  • Before you deploy — what healthcare leaders should put in place before turning agentic AI loose on their systems

Key Takeaways

  • An AI agent is only as safe as the access it inherits. Govern the identity and the data, not just the prompt.
  • The least-watched risk is the non-human one — the service accounts and tokens agents run on, which almost no one is monitoring.
  • Data-centric security is the foundation. Knowing where your sensitive data is and what can reach it is what agentic AI has to stand on.

Frameworks & Ideas Mentioned

  • Agentic AI and autonomous tool use as an emerging attack surface
  • Non-human / machine identities and least-privilege access for agents
  • Data-centric security and governance — knowing where PHI lives and what can reach it
  • AI-native data security for the cloud and GenAI era

About Pranava Adduri

Pranava Adduri is Co-Founder and CTO of Bedrock Security, an AI-native data security and governance platform built for the cloud and GenAI era. Before Bedrock, he was a founding engineer and engineering manager at Rubrik and a software development manager at Amazon Web Services, where he worked on securing large-scale data systems for Fortune 500 organizations. His focus is defending an organization’s most critical data against modern adversaries — increasingly including autonomous AI.
