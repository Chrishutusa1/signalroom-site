Who Is Governing Your AI?

with Dr. Latha Karthigaa · November 18, 2026 · 67 min

Watch the full episode

AI Governance in Practice

What AI tools are already being used inside your organization, and who has the authority to approve them? Dr. Latha Karthigaa joins Chris Hutchins to explain how AI governance becomes everyday work.

Latha discusses AI inventories, employee policies, data quality, human review, and the handoff from legal requirements to developers. The conversation also examines the skills people can bring from security, data, business analysis, and other roles, and the questions boards need to ask.

Dr. Latha Karthigaa is Director and Head of AI Governance at the Global AI Certification Council.

In this episode

  • Discover the AI tools, models, and use cases already in use.
  • Make employee policy and human review part of the workflow.
  • Connect legal, technical, and leadership responsibilities.
  • Build on the skills already present in the organization.
