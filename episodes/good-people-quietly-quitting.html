Good People Are Quietly Quitting And Your Company Is the Reason

with Carly Caminiti

Why healthcare's best people are quietly disengaging, and what leadership development has to do with retaining them. Carly Caminiti on coaching, burnout, and the leadership gaps that AI adoption will only widen.

Show Notes

Why healthcare's best people are quietly disengaging, and what leadership development has to do with retaining them. Carly Caminiti is an ICF-certified executive and personal development coach who helps leaders and teams perform at their best without burning out the people they depend on most.

Healthcare executives are moving too fast to notice who is falling behind. Their strongest performers are quietly drowning and will not say so. Leaders promoted for clinical or technical skill were never taught how to actually lead, and entire teams are absorbing the cost. At the top, there is often no one safe to talk to. These are not soft problems. They show up in turnover, missed communication, disengaged teams, and leaders too overwhelmed to drive the kind of change AI adoption demands.

Topics covered: leadership development and executive coaching in healthcare, quiet quitting and workforce retention, burnout and its operational cost, the 5C Leadership Performance System, communication and team performance, what AI adoption demands from leadership, and the ROI of investing in the people behind the strategy.
