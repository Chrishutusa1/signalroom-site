Can We Actually Trust AI With Our Health?

with Chris Bruner · Founder & CEO, Simple AI Solutions

AI can make a business more efficient, but the harder question is whether the system deserves trust. Chris Bruner explains what done-for-you AI should deliver, how poor data and technical debt can sink an initiative, and why human judgment becomes more valuable as automation expands.

Show Notes

AI can make a business more efficient, but the harder question is whether the system deserves trust. Chris Bruner explains what done-for-you AI should deliver, how poor data and technical debt can sink an initiative, and why human judgment becomes more valuable as automation expands.

What We Cover

  • What a done-for-you AI system should deliver
  • How to choose useful automation opportunities
  • Why data quality and technical debt matter
  • The human skills AI cannot supply
  • Trust, accountability, stewardship, and legacy

Key Takeaways

  • Automate the right constraint. Useful AI begins with a real workflow bottleneck, not a tool looking for a problem.
  • Trust is operational. Data quality, technical debt, training, and clear responsibility determine whether an automated system deserves confidence.
  • Human skills become more valuable. Communication, judgment, integrity, and stewardship are the capabilities automation cannot supply for a leader.

About Chris Bruner

Chris Bruner is Founder & CEO, Simple AI Solutions. Learn more at https://simpleai.solutions/ or connect on LinkedIn.
