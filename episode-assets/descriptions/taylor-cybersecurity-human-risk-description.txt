TEASER: Even flawless security controls can't fix human behavior, and in healthcare AI that's exactly where the real risk lives.

DESCRIPTION:
The biggest cybersecurity risk in healthcare AI isn't the model, it's the people using it. No firewall stops a rushed clinician from clicking the wrong link. Chris Hutchins sits down with Craig Taylor, a CISSP with 30+ years in cybersecurity, co-founder of Cyberhoot, and a virtual CISO, to reframe security around human behavior instead of bigger punishments. Craig explains why punishing employees for clicks breeds apathy while positive reinforcement and gamified phishing simulations actually change behavior. He walks through a real telehealth-style "click-fix" attack, contrasts two breaches where a "see something, say something" culture contained the damage in 30 minutes while a fear-driven one let it fester for weeks, and unpacks how locking down AI just pushes staff to shadow AI on their phones. You'll also hear why AI-powered spear phishing has erased the old warning signs, the case for password managers and short "TLDR" policies, and his simple PAR rule: Pause, Assess, Report.
