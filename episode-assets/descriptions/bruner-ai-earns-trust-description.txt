TEASER: The more you automate, the more your human judgment matters. Chris Bruner on why trust, not tools, wins in AI.

DESCRIPTION:
Automate the routine parts of a business and something counterintuitive happens: the human skills become more valuable, not less. Chris Hutchins sits down with Chris Bruner, founder and CEO of Simple AI Solutions, to unpack how small-to-mid-sized businesses can actually put AI to work. Bruner explains what a "done-for-you" AI system really looks like, how he builds scalable cold-email outreach that stays personal and value-first, and why speed-to-lead and AI agents that pre-qualify prospects and book meetings can move the needle. The conversation digs into the biggest fear owners have, an AI that misrepresents their brand, and how careful training keeps it on the rails. They also cover where owners must keep hands on the wheel and never let AI become their brain, the extra caution healthcare and sensitive data demand, and Bruner's take on legacy, stewardship, and teaching his 10-year-old son entrepreneurial responsibility.
