TEASER: The easy part is getting an AI agent to answer. The hard part is stopping it from leaking patient data on the way there.

DESCRIPTION:
Deploying AI agents inside a medical practice is one job. Keeping them from going off the rails in high-stakes care is another entirely. Travis Garland, CEO of Kickstand AI and co-founder of My Compliance Citadel, has spent 20-plus years in healthcare operations and now lives on both sides of that line. He and host Chris Hutchins get concrete about where agents genuinely help versus where they just shift the work around: front-desk scheduling and ambient transcription, staff quietly pasting schedules full of PHI into public chatbots, and physicians re-doing summaries the EHR got wrong. Travis explains why agents "drift" from their governance over time, and how a control layer that enforces at the point of action, logs verdicts to a tamper-proof audit chain, and scores each agent like a FICO score changes the game. They also dig into the patchwork of HIPAA, the EU AI Act, and state-by-state rules, plus the culture and trust questions leaders can't outsource.
