Why AI Governance Is a Human Problem — Arnaud Saint-Paul | The Signal Room EP46

Everyone's racing to govern the AI model. Almost no one is governing the thing underneath it — themselves. In this episode, serial entrepreneur Arnaud Saint-Paul (Founder & CEO of Tapuat / Heart Leadership Institute) makes a provocative case: scale AI on top of a dysfunctional, fragmented organization and you don't fix the dysfunction — you automate it and multiply it by ten.

Host Chris Hutchins presses Arnaud on what "coherence" and self-governance actually mean, why they have to come before AI adoption, and why the stakes are highest in healthcare — where fragmented leadership doesn't just cost money, it costs trust. Arnaud also shares his Heart Leadership Index, a framework that maps leadership across seven dimensions (he even ran it through ChatGPT and Claude and watched the models disagree).

In this conversation:
• Why AI is an accelerator that multiplies a leader's existing challenges by 10x
• What "coherence" really means — and the orchestra-before-the-conductor metaphor
• How a fragmented leader guarantees a fragmented AI rollout
• Why soft skills become the only skills once AI handles the hard ones
• What to actually do first, before you scale
