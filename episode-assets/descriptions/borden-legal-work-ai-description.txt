TEASER: A former intelligence analyst turned AI lawyer on why ~80% of legal work is better done by AI — and how to govern it.

DESCRIPTION:
Bennett Borden has spent his career at the intersection of intelligence analysis, law, data science, and AI governance — and he has the data to back a provocative claim. After analyzing roughly ten years of time entries from some 14,000 attorneys, he concluded that about 80% of what a lawyer does is better done by AI. Now founder and CEO of Clarion AI Partners, Borden joins Chris Hutchins to unpack what that means far beyond the law firm.

They explore what AI governance actually looks like beyond the policy document, why "supersuiting" your people — think Iron Man, not the Terminator — beats replacing them, and how the common-law standard of reasonableness keeps AI defensible even while regulation stays unsettled. Borden also digs into his public-interest work on child safety and access to justice, the idea of constitutional AI and "forming the psychology" of systems, and why 80% of the AI challenge is really a human and change-management problem.
