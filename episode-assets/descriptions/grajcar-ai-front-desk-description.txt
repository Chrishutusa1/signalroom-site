TEASER: Up to half of patient calls go unanswered. What if the front desk could pick up every one without hiring a single person?

DESCRIPTION:
The most under-discussed failure in healthcare isn't clinical, it's the phone that never gets picked up. Chris Hutchins talks with Jakub Grajcar, head of marketing at Talkie AI, whose conversational voice agents handle more than 300,000 patient conversations a month across 300-plus practices. They dig into why patient access is so often invisible, how an AI receptionist works as a first line of defense that triages calls, schedules end-to-end, and hands off to humans the moment it senses distress or severe pain. Jakub explains the discipline behind it: greetings that disclose the AI and point emergencies to 911, least-privilege EHR access under SOC 2 Type 2, and a philosophy of removing work rather than shuffling it into spreadsheets. The conversation also covers after-hours and on-call coverage, change management and patient education, why this is a supplement and not a replacement for the front desk, and where memory and multimodal AI are headed next.
