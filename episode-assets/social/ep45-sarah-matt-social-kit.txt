BODY: Dr. Sarah Matt, MD, MBA, has been a surgeon, a health-tech executive, and the person in the room when the deal gets signed. She calls the pattern "pilotitis": the org that runs pilot after pilot and never scales. In this episode she and host Chris Hutchins break down why the tech is the easy part, how to define the problem before you buy the tool, and what to lock into a contract before the clinical voice leaves the room.
