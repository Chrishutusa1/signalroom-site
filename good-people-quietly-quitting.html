Good People Are Quietly Quitting And Your Company Is the Reason

with Carly Caminiti

Executive coach Carly Caminiti reveals why 80% of employees are disengaged and your best people leave without warning. Quiet quitting, burnout, leadership development, and what AI adoption demands from healthcare leaders.

Show Notes

Most leaders think their team is fine. The surveys look good. The numbers check out. But beneath the surface, good people are silently checking out and the company culture is pushing them there.

In this episode of The Signal Room, Chris Hutchins sits down with executive coach Carly Caminiti to uncover the hidden workforce crisis that no one is talking about. They break down why 80% of employees are disengaged, why your best people leave without warning, and why adding more AI and technology is making the burnout problem worse, not better.

Carly Caminiti is an ICF-certified executive and personal development coach and creator of the 5C Leadership Performance System, a 12-week program that helps leadership teams reduce burnout, build trust, and actually keep their best people.

What You Will Learn

→ Why staff satisfaction surveys are hiding the real truth

→ The real cost of replacing one good employee (it is not just money)

→ How burnout spreads silently across entire teams

→ Why good technical people make bad managers and what to do about it

→ The one assumption every leader needs to stop making today

→ Why AI is actually pushing us back toward human connection
