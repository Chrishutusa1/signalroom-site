Why Most Healthcare AI Never Delivers ROI

Parth Gargish with host Chris Hutchins — 26 minutes

About this video

Parth Gargish joins Chris Hutchins to separate healthcare AI hype from measurable value. The conversation examines ROI-first use cases, the people-process-tools framework, workforce trust, and why mid-level managers determine whether an AI roadmap becomes operational reality.

Topics covered

  • Why useful AI starts with a measurable business or clinical outcome
  • How small automation wins can create durable operational value
  • Why people and process readiness come before tools
  • How leaders can address workforce fear without minimizing it
