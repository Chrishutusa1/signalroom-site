AI Readiness: Why 85% of Healthcare Organizations Aren't Ready

Ratnadeep Bhattacharjee with host Chris Hutchins · 33 minutes

About this video

Ratnadeep Bhattacharjee joins Chris Hutchins to explain why AI readiness is fundamentally a data-readiness problem. They unpack a four-lens audit for completeness, consistency, connectivity, and compliance and show why fixing the foundation often creates value before a model is deployed.

Topics covered

  • The four-lens data-readiness audit for healthcare AI
  • Why fragmented EHR and claims data undermine AI projects
  • How interoperability and patient-360 architecture support trusted decisions
  • Why data maturity must be an ongoing organizational capability
