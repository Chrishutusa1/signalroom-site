Your CISO Can't Govern What They Can't See | The Shadow Formulary Problem

Dr. Ömer Atlı with host Chris Hutchins — 37 minutes

About this video

Emergency physician and AI red-teamer Dr. Ömer Atlı joins Chris Hutchins to examine why models that pass academic benchmarks can fail at 3 a.m. in resource-constrained care. Their conversation shows why banning unapproved AI does not govern it and how clinical safety leaders can test the tools already in use.

Topics covered

  • Why unapproved consumer AI has become a shadow clinical formulary
  • How lethal omissions differ from obvious hallucinations
  • Why clinical AI must understand real staffing, transport, and resource constraints
  • How CISOs and clinical safety officers can govern tools nobody procured
