The Compliance Illusion: Why Written AI Policies Fail in Seconds

Aaron Puckett with host Chris Hutchins — 50 minutes

About this video

Aaron Puckett joins Chris Hutchins to expose the gap between written AI policies and the technical controls needed to enforce them. The conversation covers shadow AI, data loss prevention, identity access, compliance evidence, and the speed of AI-enabled cyberattacks.

Topics covered

  • Why policy without enforceable infrastructure creates a compliance illusion
  • How shadow AI can expose PHI, customer data, and intellectual property
  • The role of DLP, identity controls, configuration management, and human oversight
  • Why cyber insurance and audit readiness depend on provable execution
