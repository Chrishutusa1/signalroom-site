Why 50% of New Nurses Quit Within 2 Years | Angela Millan

Angela Millan with host Chris Hutchins — 50 minutes

About this video

Nurse practitioner, educator, and Prismn Health Technologies co-founder Angela Millan joins Chris Hutchins to examine the readiness gap facing new nurses. Their conversation covers the limits of NCLEX preparation, the cost of abbreviated orientation, and why AI should help nurses rehearse judgment rather than make clinical decisions for them.

Topics covered

  • Why passing the NCLEX does not guarantee bedside readiness
  • How clinical knowledge differs from clinical judgment under pressure
  • Why short orientations and weak mentorship accelerate burnout and attrition
  • How Nurse Hazel builds reasoning without replacing the nurse
