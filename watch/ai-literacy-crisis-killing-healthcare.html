A Doctor Warns: Stop Reading Your Own Lab Results Using AI

Dr. Steven Labkoff with host Chris Hutchins · 48 minutes

About this video

Dr. Steven Labkoff joins Chris Hutchins to explain why reading personal lab results through general-purpose AI can lead to a confident answer that is clinically wrong. Their conversation examines the healthcare AI literacy gap, the danger of removing clinical context from medical data, and why leaders must treat AI as information technology rather than magic.

Topics covered

  • Why AI interpretations of lab results can sound credible while missing essential clinical context
  • How over-trusting general-purpose AI can contribute to the dumbing down of medicine
  • Why healthcare AI literacy is a governance and patient-safety discipline
  • What earlier technology cycles teach leaders about evaluating AI claims
