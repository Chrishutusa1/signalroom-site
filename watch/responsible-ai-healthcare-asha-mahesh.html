Responsible AI in Healthcare: How Leaders Build Trust

Asha Mahesh with host Chris Hutchins — 22 minutes

About this video

Asha Mahesh joins Chris Hutchins to explore responsible AI inside pharmaceutical and healthcare environments. The conversation connects ethical leadership, clinical-trial innovation, workforce trust, human-centered adoption, and the guardrails required when patients and treatments are on the line.

Topics covered

  • Why human impact must remain the north star for healthcare AI
  • How ethics becomes a way of working rather than a policy
  • What COVID vaccine development teaches about mission-aligned AI
  • Why clinical accountability requires meaningful human oversight
