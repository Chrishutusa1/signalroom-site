Healthcare Cybersecurity and Human Risk

Craig Taylor with host Chris Hutchins · 58 minutes

About this video

Craig Taylor joins Chris Hutchins to explain why cybersecurity is fundamentally a human behavior problem, how AI makes social engineering more believable, and why positive reinforcement builds stronger security habits than punishment.

Topics covered

  • Why punishment-based security awareness programs fail
  • How social engineering exploits urgency, trust, and distraction
  • What generative AI changes about phishing and impersonation
  • Why psychological safety improves incident reporting
  • Approved tools, patching, data reduction, and practical AI governance
