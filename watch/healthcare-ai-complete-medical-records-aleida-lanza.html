Healthcare AI Fails Without Complete Medical Records

Aleida Lanza with host Chris Hutchins — 16 minutes

About this video

Aleida Lanza joins Chris Hutchins to explain why healthcare AI cannot be safer or more precise than the patient history available to it. The conversation covers fragmented records, patient ownership, interoperability, claims, imaging, and the risks created when missing data remains invisible.

Topics covered

  • Why patient portals expose only a fraction of the complete record
  • How missing data becomes invisible model bias
  • Why claims and imaging belong in the AI data foundation
  • How patient-owned records support safer precision medicine
