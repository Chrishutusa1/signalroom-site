Why Lawyers Should Be Terrified of This AI Stat

Bennett Borden with host Chris Hutchins — 50 minutes

About this video

Former intelligence analyst, lawyer, and data scientist Bennett Borden joins Chris Hutchins to unpack the finding that roughly 80% of recurring legal work could be handled more effectively with AI-assisted workflows. They explore reasonableness, defensibility, constitutional AI, and why governance can accelerate responsible adoption.

Topics covered

  • What ten years of time entries from 14,000 attorneys revealed
  • Why the right adoption model is Iron Man, not Terminator
  • How reasonableness and defensibility protect organizations as regulation changes
  • Why the hardest part of AI adoption is psychological rather than technical
