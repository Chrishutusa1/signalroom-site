The Silent Way AI is Destroying Corporate Trust | Katherine Tuominen

Katherine Tuominen with host Chris Hutchins — 47 minutes

About this video

Organizational narrative strategist Katherine Tuominen joins Chris Hutchins to explain why AI adoption is a human trust event. They explore narrative debt, generic AI-generated communication, psychological safety, data leakage into public models, and the gap between executive messaging and frontline experience.

Topics covered

  • How narrative debt grows when leadership messages contradict lived experience
  • Why AI slop and automated email loops weaken authentic communication
  • How hidden frontline workarounds create privacy and compliance exposure
  • What psychological safety and intrinsic motivation require from leaders
