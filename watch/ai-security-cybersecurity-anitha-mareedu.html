AI Security Is Expanding the Attack Surface

Anitha Mareedu with host Chris Hutchins — 19 minutes

About this video

Anitha Mareedu joins Chris Hutchins to map the security risks that arrive with AI deployment. The conversation covers application security, APIs, sensitive information, the CIA triad, industry-specific priorities, national exposure, and why continuous learning is now part of every cybersecurity role.

Topics covered

  • How AI enlarges the modern application attack surface
  • Why autonomous access changes blast-radius calculations
  • How healthcare balances confidentiality and availability
  • Why security knowledge must evolve as quickly as AI systems
