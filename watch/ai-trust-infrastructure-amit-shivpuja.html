You Can't Scale AI Without Scaling Trust

Amit Shivpuja with host Chris Hutchins — 15 minutes

About this video

Amit Shivpuja joins Chris Hutchins to examine the hidden infrastructure beneath trustworthy AI. The conversation covers data quality, lineage, subject-matter expertise, monitoring, workforce trust, and the human verification required when agentic systems change over time.

Topics covered

  • Why poor data becomes “garbage squared” in AI systems
  • How early subject-matter expertise prevents late failure
  • Why monitoring agentic AI is continuous work
  • How human verification turns governance into operational trust
